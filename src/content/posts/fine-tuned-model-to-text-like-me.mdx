|---|---|---|
| "abe kaha tha kal raat" | "Arre wah! Kal raat ka scene yaad hai? 😂🤣..." | "sone de bkl" |

that is an actual output the fine-tuned model produced and i love it so much. i'd actually say that to a friend — it's short, chaotic, sweet. just *muah*.

but obviously i wouldn't just talk to friends. i've got family, elder cousins, younger cousins, colleagues, people from dating apps and whatnot on insta — so i can't have the model respond the same way to everyone. had to process the data properly and ngl, that was my favorite part.

i actually did the same project back in 2023, whenever Llama 3 8B was the shit. fine-tuned it, it was great — the inference was laggy, the output was meh, there was no Claude Code to help me. few years and actual deployment experience later, i wondered: why not fix everything i know was broken, improve what i can, do it for free this time around, and actually deploy the damn thing.

so here we are.

## why cloning voice is harder than it looks

simple reason: me and 1 friend shared 7k messages and reels whereas me and my family/extended family shared less than a thousand. the data is skewed, so very skewed.

also more than half of my replies are to meme posts or reels. the old version skipped reels entirely, so the model didn't even register my personality — let alone my personality with respect to different subsets of people.

obviously a human talks to everyone differently, but to simplify the math i divided the people i talked to into:
1. family — sisters, mom, dad
2. elders — massi, uncles, cousins
3. younger siblings — bhanje, bhatije and all that
4. close friends — basically can say any unhinged thought to them
5. friends/acquaintances — hi-hello people
6. bumble matches — the model's gotta have my rizz, bro (cries internally)

to have the model understand how to talk to each subset, i first had to figure out how much data i actually had. thankfully only elders and younger siblings were lacking.

the fix: added some synthetic data myself, then fed it to Nvidia's latest free model on OpenRouter and manually selected what i'd say in each context. and boom — data increased. it didn't fix the issue completely (still had to do DPO for these subsets) but the overall skew ratio went from 52:1 to something the current results are great at.

## the data

thousands of messages, 100s of DMs, 10s of problems.

the biggest one: how do you insert the context of a reel into the training data?

as usual, long live open source — yt-dlp to download and get metadata like captions, ffmpeg to extract frames, free models to describe what the frames show, Whisper-medium to transcribe the audio. boom — you have the full reel context: what the meme shows, what the audio says, what song was used, everything.

obviously running this whole pipeline on every single reel was dumb, so i used an open model to judge whether the auto-caption was already good enough — if yes, skip the pipeline. after removing great captions and dead links, i got to 587 reels that went through the pipeline, of which 500-ish got selected due to high/mid confidence. the rest i manually labeled.

## the gpu saga

ok so enough about how it's all free — here's exactly how each part ran:

1. data processing pipeline — done overnight on a free Kaggle T4
2. the model i chose to fine-tune (Qwen3.5) doesn't support training on T4 — weak GPU, less VRAM, can't use bf16
3. free $15 credits from Lightning.ai came to the rescue — used an L4 for SFT and DPO of the 4B version, didn't like the coherence, did the same on 9B
4. now comes inference
5. adapters (all 4: 4B-SFT, 4B-DPO, 9B-SFT, 9B-DPO) hosted for free on Hugging Face
6. backend lives on Modal (free $30 credits)

## proving it wasn't just a prompt

after the whole thing i wondered if simple prompt engineering could give me the same results. thankfully, it doesn't.

gave the base model 4 of my actual messages as in-context examples — best-case few-shot, its best shot at sounding like me. fine-tune got zero examples. fine-tune still won 2x (reply-only perplexity: 30.6 vs 14.9).

a smarter base just becomes a better generic chatbot. the fine-tune collapses all that into one specific terse shape.

## the cuss problem

data is friend-heavy so the model cusses by default. to everyone. including family.

DPO fixed the elder/family leak (2% → 0%) but made it too clean with close friends too --> alignment tax, you fix one thing you break another.

so instead of one compromised model: two adapters on one base. SFT for close friends (crude, real). DPO for everyone else (clean). hot-swap at inference based on who you're talking to. 0% leak AND unfiltered close-friend mode simultaneously.
