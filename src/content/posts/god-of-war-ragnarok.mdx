I fucking love God of War — the whole series, but i specifically adore the latest 2 entries. Peak of any media, imho. GoW 2018 was better than any movie i watched, any book i read — and Ragnarok somehow made it better.

from combat to story to side missions, to acting, directing, cinematography — everything is peak. from a technical perspective as well, both games are phenomenal. i still remember the first scene of 2018, the one where Kratos places his hand on the log, and i was thinking damn, looks as real as my hand — like, it's a game, and a base PS4 runs it? crazy optimisation.

also the Leviathan Axe is easily top 3 weapons in gaming, and i know because i've been playing PC games since 2008. the way it comes back to your hand and how weighty it feels in combat is awesome. but what makes it even better as an art form is the story, characters, and art design — each realm feels worth exploring, each character worth listening to, each storyline worth following.

i can't remember being pissed at pixels ever — but i was at Odin after he killed Brok (all 0s and 1s btw), didn't go to sleep until i killed the old tyrant.

i love each character deeply but my favs are Laufey, Mimir, Baldur, and Thor.

## Laufey

Faye is my favorite part of GoW 2018. she wasn't even in the game — you just see Kratos burning her body at the start, that's it, no flashbacks, no nothing. yet the way she controlled the "Path" her husband and child took was incredible. she protected and guided them until they reached their goal.

her impact was even greater in Ragnarok, because this time she actually had dialogue and scenes in flashbacks. (sidenote — crazy cinematography: no cuts, but there are flashbacks and character switches, all seamless, like wtf.)

i'm gonna be honest — her scenes are my favorite part of the entire game. i enjoy the combat and big bad bosses and world building and all the realms, but sometimes you just want to see the woman who tamed the beast. also each of her segments is placed at a point where Kratos is at a new low, thinking all hope is lost — then she comes and guides him. in a way she was the reason the prophecy failed and Kratos and co succeeded. her last flashback saved Kratos and helped him let Atreus be himself. absolute cinema.

when she said "To grieve deeply is to have loved fully" i damn near cried. it's still my bio on some accounts to this day and i don't think it's changing anytime soon lol.

## why i named a project after him

now comes Mimir — the smartest man in all the universe, one of the many reasons i named my personal assistant after him. the way Santa Monica Studios broke down his myths and made him a helpful companion is incredibly creative.

he gives callouts, entertains during the "boring" boat rides, and his presence alone is enough to deliver any lore to Kratos — instead of the tired video game cliché of protagonists monologuing to themselves.

my favorite boat story is Thamur's. the way Mimir narrates the story while you're actively searching for the tip of the chisel that killed the giant whose story you're hearing, all while rowing your boat towards that giant's dead body — awesome. the timing with the reveal was phenomenal. the way Mimir says "see for yourself," then 2 seconds later you see this massive giant lying on snow-covered terrain, dead — with the very chisel you're looking for.

goosebumps.

a knowledgeable thing permanently attached to the thing that acts — that's the whole point. every agent framework wants a Mimir. so when i was building [my own](./mimir-home-assistant), the name was obvious.

thinking of replaying it — 9th time — before GoW: Laufey, but have to finish my backlog first.
